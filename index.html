MANIFESTO

We don’t sell transformation. We sell the hours nobody sees.

No highlights. No shortcuts. A room, a bar, a coach who is paying attention.

PHILOSOPHY

The grip decides everything else.

Chalk is the last honest test before the bar moves. If the grip fails, the plan was fiction. We coach from the contact point outward — hands, bracing, position — so the load is earned instead of negotiated.

PROGRAMS

Two ways to train here.

Both blocks are coached, progressive and written for real schedules. They differ in temperature: one is weight, the other is recovery.
